**TL;DR - Console issues fixes are coming, balance changes are coming, Season 2 will have a new map, lore being not a priority right now, communication and social features are a priority right now, vehicle missions getting rebalanced soon, low player count addressed, info on replays, other smaller stuff.**

For a full disclosure, this post has been reviewed (and potentially received some corrections) by Mr.Matcha before becoming public.

Disclaimer: if you don’t see a question that interests you, there is a high probability that this question was not asked during the stream.

Repeated questions, reiterated public information and vague answers were not included in the episode.

This episode covers **two** Wednesday streams, **29th of July** and **5th of August. **

And making these things, that you want to buy; this is the challenge to at least to this in a short term. But, and I know this is not going to be applicable for you; for people who joined the game later on, we will also be giving them an opportunity to use their surplus intel to complete past battle passes. Not one from Season 0, but other ones. Again, more on that, Soon™ 

10.
Q: **Can we address the elephant in the room pls? Player count is so low that sometimes half the team are Bots. Any roadmap or plans you have going forward. I’m only asking because I care about the game. Thx.**
A: (Mr.Conway) Obviously player counts would be great to have higher, but we are very aware of the fact that we have quite a bit of work to do. Changes that you guys have requested, QoL things that we needed to ship, we’re also approaching the end of Season 1, so a lot of people have finished the content. Then there is an unconscionable heat wave ripping through the majority of where our player base is situated, and so all of these things kinda of play together. At the moment we have a little bit low numbers, but that’s absolutely fine, and we fully anticipate that this is going to change. Don’t worry.

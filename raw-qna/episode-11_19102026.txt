**TL;DR - Lots of talk about tested role changes, module rework feedback is taken to heart and will not arrive in Season 3, ranked is probably going to be based on 5v5 modes, devs improved their pipelines and integrated community team into it to faster address balancing issues, A2 is def getting a nerf one way or another, new tracking system being implemented, some KC discussion. **

For a full disclosure, this post has been reviewed (and potentially received some corrections) by Lynx before becoming public.

Disclaimer: if you don’t see a question that interests you, there is a high probability that this question was not asked during the stream.

Repeated questions, reiterated public information and vague answers were not included in the episode.

1.
Q: **What people can expect from this Roles update?**
A: (Hedrik) So, for Season 3, it is the Role Identity Feature - a thing that brings more unique gameplay to every role that we have in the game. They were there since release - defenders, assaults, marksman. We’re introducing a fourth, which I think is, from my side, the most exciting one; which is - support, and a really clear thing for you to focus on when you're playing them in a game. Together with some updates to what kind of roles a vehicle is assigned to specific agents.

2.
Q: **It feels like a significant change that’s coming, and will shake up things quite a lot.**
A: (Hedrik) In terms of the tanks - they stay roughly the same, but indeed, the bigger shape-up is how you will use them. So it will drive your objective in a match, mostly. So, if you’re playing as an assault - you will maybe try a few different things now, than you did before. As a marksman - you might put yourself in slightly different positions. The tanks you know - they are staying as you like them, but it’s mostly about the game objectives.

3.
Q: **So healer tanks? I’d like a healer tank.**
A: (Hedrik) So, supports in our game are going to be a bit more dynamic. So, the support element is something that we saw as an opportunity in our game. There are tanks that are suitable for it, so first of all - why not? The other thing is also - there is quite often cases where the fight can get quite heated. You might go down low [hp] and having someone to come in and save the day, generally for the person holding the line is good to have, but also being able to do that is just fun. So, that’s something that we want to explore, and we built on that.

4.
Q: **When will the better MM come and the better Map rotation? Do you have any information?**
A: (Mr.Conway) So we're going to make a couple of adjustments to game modes rotation with the upcoming update, which I can spoiler - is coming next week. And then we're going to make a few more changes to this with the start of Season 3. Match making is not like a “one and done - we make a change”, right? This is something that we’re always keeping an eye on, we’re trying to see what we can tweak and how.

5.
Q: **How will the new roles system encourage players? So for example they will decide to support and heal me, instead of staying behind and sniping as usual?**
A: (Hedrik) Well, the bigger thing is also that in order to support - you’ll need to close in. Get up close to someone, and the reason to do it - is its part of the core gameplay now. So, you will receive a score for doing so. You're not just doing it for them, you’re also doing it for yourself. Plus, when you do [support] - you will heal yourself a little bit as well. So, if you’re getting low, there is a reason to help one of your allies. You both benefit.

6.
Q: **What’s the design goal behind adding abilities to whole classes, when vehicles in the same class play very differently? And why active abilities rather than smaller passive bonuses? **
A: (Hedrik) Part of the answer is actually in the question. Some of the tanks that play in the same role - play very differently, so it’s hard to read what their exact purpose is, in a match, on how to win the game, actually. So we wanted to better define this, so if you are talking about a defender, what we’re doing is - we’re giving them a means to really lock down a specific area. And that is what defenders are focused on doing. But while they’re locking down the area, there might be an assault who can get them out of that position. And generally while they’re trying to hold down that position, someone else might come in to help them out. So, the goal for what you are trying to do in a match - becomes much clearer, that is why we call it “the role”, like that’s what you’re driving in order for your team to win. That is the main goal, but the side effect of that is that it creates a certain new layer of dynamic gameplay, where, because you are understanding very clearly what your opponent is trying to achieve, you can counter something to that play. Of course, some tanks play differently, that is where they bring in the unique element to help achieve it.

7.
Q: **When will the rest of the changes for the roles be announced? They concern me deeply in both their execution and justification, but hopefully it'll make more sense when the rest of the changes come out.**
A: (Mr.Conway) So, we are going to be publishing an article going a little bit more into depth as to what exactly we’re doing and why, tomorrow (15/10/2026) and we are also tomorrow launching our LSP test for specifically this feature, so we can get your feedback, your input, and make any changes that need to be made before the feature actually goes live.
(Hedrik) The feedback on the LSP is something that we’re looking forward to and monitor it closely.

8.
Q: **Can we get an overview of what vehicles will be in what class?**
A: (Hedrik) Bradley will be support. Leo 1A6 stays marksman. A lot of things stay very close to how they were because, as some of you already pointed out - they are fitting to the role. But, for example, for the support there are some changes in there for Bradley. One of the bigger changes there is that some agents have vehicles of different roles. So, right now, the agent very much defines the role, but this is moving to the vehicle. So, with the swapping, you’re able to switch your role in a match.
(Mr.Conway also confirmed that the full list of changes, including changes to roles, will be announced in the article)

9.
Q: **Will agents and vehicles feel unique with class abilities on top of the move to the generic manufactured modules (module rework)? How do you keep agents and vehicles distinct? **
A: (Hedrik) So, one of the things is that even though there is a role and that is defined for the overall class - we still want to offer diversity within that role, right? Like you want to find a way that you like one thing more than you like another. And the abilities actually help you achieve those roles. So, the unique elements are not the M1E1 right? It has the same ability as any other defender, like the T-72. But in order to play it, you have unique abilities that help you achieve it, create a situation that maybe works better so to how you like to play. So, the difference is very much still there, just, as I was talking about it earlier; it’s very much about the objectives that you’re trying to drive for your team, so it’s coming for the team play element, but the uniqueness of the vehicle is definitely something that we want to keep in that.

10.
Q: **Could you explain the marksman’s “Unseen” bonus? What does “Unseen” mean in this case? **
A: (Hedrik) So, there isn’t a very specific bonus to being unseen, but for the marksman it will be important to remain unseen. So, in our game, if you see the tank - you can shoot it. And for marksmen it is something that we want to highlight even more. So, you get a bonus, if you get the sneaky shot off on your enemy. But in order to achieve that, you need to make sure that they are not shooting at you. So you cannot just have a one stand-off and reap the benefits of the quite strong bonus that the marksman has. Basically, if you are remaining invisible to the enemies, coming up from angles that they don’t know where you’re at, you can take the time and actually get your surgical shot off. However, if you are spotted at any time, or you take any fire (just a hit for example, not necessarily damage) - you lose your bonus. The benefit is very much driven from how you create the opportunity yourself. What we want to reinforce with a lot of these abilities is that you don’t have to rely on what your enemy does, as you create your own opportunities. I think one of the comparisons that is maybe interesting, is like a sneak attack in Dungeons and Dragons, as there are several ways that as a Rogue, you can do this. And that is also generally how I like to approach it, as a marksman. If the enemy is occupied, I’ll be able to snipe at them. If they are just traversing between A and B, they don’t know that I’m there - I can get sneaky shots on them, and that makes the marksman more unique.

11.
Q: **Any news about the module rework or is it still on the back burner and won't be implemented yet?**
A: (Hedrik) So, we tested that recently, we’re taking the feedback to heart, it means that also we need to evaluate what is actually there. For now, we’re putting the priority towards the roles, so that will come up first, and we will come back with news later as to what we’re doing with the modules. So, don’t expect that [module rework] for the release of S3.

12.
Q: **How does support actually play? Do supports have to choose between healing and shooting? Like a heal beam healer in other hero games or can they fire while healing? And will vehicles that change role also get new modules and abilities? **
A: (Hedrik)  So, what you will need to do is - you will need to move up to your ally that you want to heal, and you need to be in close proximity and look at them for a very short period of time. So you need to be really up close to them, and you will trigger the heal on them, they will recover health. But you are support, you still a tank that we have in the game, with all the unique elements. You still have abilities, you still have your rate of fire and your gun. You can do all of that. We don’t want to take any of that away from the things that you love about the tank that you have a certain attachment to and that you’re playing. So, while you might be supporting your ally, you’re still there in the fight with them. You can use your abilities, if you have abilities to disable your opponents, maybe that’s another good way to support. So there is all kinds of different flair that can be added on top of what you’re bringing to the battle as a support.

13.
Q: **Is Fuzzer actually moving to Support or was the Marder 1A3 just a placeholder in the video? **
A: (Hedrik) So, Fuzzer as an agent is no longer defined as the specific role. We’re exploring different ways to play with it. What I can say is that what we’re testing right now is that the Bradley will be support. Marder - potentially…? There are still other options where we’re also leaving it as a marksman. For the upcoming LSP there is a limited roster that we want to test. We’ll learn from it, track the feedback, and adapt if necessary.

14.
Q:** Which other agents or vehicles will change role and could one agent end up with vehicles in different roles? **
A: (Hedrik) Yes, the roles will be decided based on the vehicle, no longer on the agent. There will be agents that have 2 vehicles of the same role. There will be more agents actually that have vehicles of different roles. For example, M1E1 is a defender, but the FV is actually a support. Close range support. Very different from how you would approach it with the Bradley, but both of them, their goal is to assist their allies.

15.
Q: **Hi. Right now, this is the only game in the tank setting with normal gameplay. You’ve often said that you’re thinking about how ranked should work. Can you roughly describe what it will be like or what modes it will be based on?**
A: (Mr.Conway) There is no decision yet on what modes will be used for a future ranked mode. Obviously we have some candidates. I would say it’s more likely that we’re going to be using 5v5 modes than 10v10 modes for this. But also as you saw with those modes that we’re still running experimentally, they haven’t fully settled, and we may still be making tweaks and adjustments to them.

16.
Q: **I believe the support class should serve as a bridge between attackers and snipers. Categorizing the FV as a support unit was a very strange decision; the FV has one of the best survivability ratings among defenders.**
A: (Hedrik) Yes, it does. Which is giving it, in this context, a very different way to how you approach supporting your allies, as you would when playing a Bradley. It’s very survivable, can help lock down locations, but in terms of being the one actually creating the problem area for your opponents - you’d be better off playing the M1E1 in that context. … The act of defending is not just necessarily actually defending a point, it’s that you’re creating a problematic area for your opponents that is away from your tank, so your tank is not necessarily the centre point. You’re projecting an area in front of you in which enemies would just not be able to pass. It works a little bit like a taunt. You will need to deal with that defender if you want to get rid of them.

17.
Q: **With multiple tanks fitting the same class but different roles under the same agent, considering the playstyle for each tank, are the "class / role" abilities be an assigned ability per class, or will we have a pool for abilities to fit each tank individually under the blanket of said role / class ?**
A: (Hedrik) One of the things that we are playing around with in terms of ideas it’s different variations, but the thing we want to nail down first is - is this signature ability really useful and can it really enhance the team play? Creating different variations would be a next step we could explore - if it would truly add more interesting and more fun gameplay. So, in terms of a pool - for now it is really just the one signature ability and the variation of that actually comes from playing a different tank within that same role.

18.
Q: **I see the reason behind FV being a support (considering it has healing bastions and jamming field), but the difference between it and Bradley is huge, will matchmaking account for that?**
A: (Hedrik) In a way, this is very much about the balance, and how we set that up. So the support ability is the main thing that as a support - you should be driving for. How can I support my allies? You would play FV much closer to your defenders because you actually have the survivability to hold that up. The downside of that is - enemies will know where you are, and they can get you out of that position, like the assault roles are actually very much specialised in this. The offset of that is the Bradley - it has some survivability, it has the ERA package, so it can mitigate some incoming damage. But it’s more mobile, so if you were to just stand next to your defender in that, you’ll be picked off quite easily. You’ll definitely be  a high priority target for any lingering marksman around that will be able to get you off the battlefield, so you want to be more mobile. What you’re doing once you’re actually healing - that’s very much the same. It’s about how you approach it that makes it different. If there is too much of a difference in how effective it is - that is the thing that we all need to address with the balancing parts, and that’s something that is actually why we are testing it on LSP. We need to see how that actually goes and adjust when necessary

19.
Q: **I believe the support class should possess both support and damage-dealing abilities, but it should have the survivability of a sniper—certainly not that of a heavy tank. The support class shouldn't be an independent class; these units need to play in conjunction with allies of other classes.**
A: (Hedrik) Yes, support should be something that you as a team want to protect and as a player want to survive as long as possible. And that is also why you want to either be robust, like it doesn’t mean that you should be unkillable, but more, like I mentioned - there’s the two different approaches to being the support in that context. The prioritization in terms of team play still stays the same. So you might have an armour package that can protect you from the front, but in terms of survivability - you are equally feeble. Especially in terms of the role play where a well-placed marksman, that you don’t know where they are; they will take out both

20.
Q: **Can support be played like an "anti carry", a big play denier similar to games like league of legends or overwatch ?**
A: (Hedrik) Well, you’ll be able to really chunk up specific allies, and it very much depends on how your team composition is set up and how you’re going to approach the overall team setup. One of the things that we want to avoid is that - it shouldn’t be a binary thing. A more mobile support might be able to flank around and actually really buff up an assault that is really countering an assault on the enemy team that is doing so, but they might be very feeble. So you can buff them up, while a more static support would be better at directly assisting the defender holding a specific position. I can even see other options where you would be a sniper support, where you are actually able to help them out while they’re taking a lot of fire, so that the marksman actually can take a lot more risks. They still need to be unseen and not be shot at, but it’s up to [player] to find ways to make that work.

21.
Q: **[long follow-up about the module rework, expressing the fact that aforementioned rework strips vehicles of their uniqueness]**
A: (Hedrik) Yeah, so that is something that we took to heart, and we’ll look at that and come back with something that addresses such feedback.

A: (Hedrik) It shouldn’t be functioning as a full-fledged defender. So that is something that we’ll look at how to set it up. And also that is, in terms of a vehicle perspective, role-based perspective - a defender will have additional benefits that FV wouldn’t have in such a situation.

23.
Q: **[Mr.Conway talks about more balance changes coming in S3 and prompting Hedrik to open-up a little about the “behind the scenes” work that they’ve been doing] **
A: (Hedrik) One of the things that we’re paying attention to, to do some work in the background for it - is just generally to improve our pipelines of how we are processing balancing changes. One of the things that we’re looking to do is smaller and more iterative changes, so we’re directly integrating the community team to better track the feedback that we’re getting and finding a way to directly translate that to changes. The more exciting thing is that - with some of the new technology that we have coming online we should be able to more quickly adapt things without necessarily having to push a very big patch. So, we want to be able to be more reactive to the feedback that you guys are sharing with us.
(Mr.Conway) Obviously we are aware of what the feedback is, we are aware of what the state of balance is in the game, but the ability to make a change quickly, without having to go through a full certification cycle for all the consoles and all the other overhead that comes with deploying a full patch. We hope it’s really going to help, and this is something that you should start seeing in effect in the next couple of weeks and months.

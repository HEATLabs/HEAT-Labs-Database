**TL;DR - Roadmap progress, future seasons potentially be longer than 2 months, Xbox crashes known, PIP for ult cinematics being considered but not gonna be added any time soon due-to engine’s current technical limitations, QoL features in the backlog, other smaller stuff. **

Surprisingly, this will be a pretty short episode.

For a full disclosure, this post has been reviewed (and potentially received some corrections) by Mr.Matcha before becoming public.

Disclaimer: if you don’t see a question that interests you, there is a high probability that this question was not asked during the stream.

Repeated questions, reiterated public information and vague answers were not included in the episode.

1.
Q: **Will this be the last patch of this season or should we expect more? I'm just curious**
A: (Mr.Conway) It is too early to talk about what’s going to be in there, but I am happy to report that we are planning to have an update 1.1.2 in a couple of weeks, where we’re going to do some more interesting stuff, but I don’t wanna give everything away before it’s confirmed, so just look out for that when it comes. But yes, there will be another update. And It also brings me to another point - please do keep the feedback coming. I really hope that we’ve shown with this QoL focused update, that we take your feedback seriously, that we’re absolutely willing to revisit our own assumptions about what’s good and bad for the game, what needs to be changed, and make changes to it, even if it can be a little bit painful. For example with the ultimate abilities - we’ve spent a lot of time making those look really cool, and for now we're gonna give some of you an option to switch them off. I think Esmatty pointed out [that] in future it would be cool to have like a Picture In Picture (PIP) solution, and maybe this is the approach that we will investigate. We’ll have to see, but for now, thank you very much for talking to us so much and please keep doing it **so that we know** what to fix next. Because for me, that’s an important question.

Q: **Still crashing on Xbox. Anything in the new patch that will help?**
A: (Remmy) It’s been reported, it’s a…. I don’t wanna say “isolated case” but it’s near “isolated case”, but it’s been reported. It is known. Thank you for reporting it in a lot of detail, which helped a lot. 

6.
Q: **Are you working on PiP instead of totally removing the cinematics?**
A: (Mr. Conway) As I was mentioning earlier, PIP is something that we are considering, but there is also a technical hurdle there to make the engine support this. So, we’ll have to see. 

7.
Q: **Why can’t we get patch fixes each week?**
A: (Mr.Conway) This is because it takes us quite a bit of work to deploy a patch and it also takes us quite a bit of time to get everything ready for said patch. So deploying something new, **every single week**, would be inefficient.

8.
Q: **Considering shortening the future seasons? Or adding more tanks in a single season**
A: (Mr.Conway) So, the main issue that we have with doing shorter seasons or adding more tanks every season - is that is takes us quite a long time to make the content for the season, and there is a hard limit on how much [content] we can realistically make in a given time frame. So, while there definitely is a chance that you will be seeing more tanks in a single season, we probably will actually have slightly longer seasons than now, **BUT**, as you saw with [update] 1.1.1 - we do also add stuff in-between [of season]. So, just because there is a season [active] - doesn’t mean that you won't get any new content within that season. 

9.
Q: **Did we talk about getting a roadmap at least for the remainder of 2026? Our player count is not great. We need more people coming in. Communication and Content. Appreciate your hard work.**
A: (Mr.Conway) We did talk about it, and I have started talking about it internally, but I can’t promise when we can do a roadmap. But we would like to! I just wanna make sure that we can stick to what’s in the roadmap, and we are still making a couple of decisions as to what some of that roadmap will be. 

10
Q: **Guys, we love the game. That’s why we’re here. Not being able to share an internal roadmap, you must have one, folks will continue to leave and it hurts. I am asking because I care and want full teams and not bots and the game to grow.**
A: (Mr.Conway) We of course have an internal roadmap, absolutely, but the tricky part is turning the internal roadmap into an external roadmap. **BUT**! That is **not** to say that we are not having conversations about this and that we are not sharing other things. But I will try.

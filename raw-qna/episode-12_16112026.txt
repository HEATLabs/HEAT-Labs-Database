**TL;DR - Raketa ult buff potentially incoming, game has a long-term plan and will not shut down, lack of players addressed - more people will be onboarded, a certain way to spend excess intel is coming in S3 - no details yet, tournament incoming, new dev talk video coming around or sometime in December, new class abilities addressed. **

For a full disclosure, this post has been reviewed (and potentially received some corrections) by Flam1ngo before becoming public.

Disclaimer: if you don’t see a question that interests you, there is a high probability that this question was not asked during the stream.

This episode covers **all PGA streams**, Wednesday streams on **21st and 28th of October**, as well as on **4th of November**, plus S3 preview stream on **12th of November. **


Repeated questions, reiterated public information and vague answers were not included in the episode.

1.
Q: **Reaper's ult takes forever to land, so it's a rare sight to see it hit someone with the initial explosion, and now with the nerf of napalm, I think its fire will be barely useful too, most tanks can go through it the short way without getting any damage. Any plans to change it already? If not, please give a look at its efficiency.**
A: (Remmy) Known issue. We are discussing how to improve it.

2.
Q: **Can we have a full dev team stream, have Artyom, Hedrik, Rickles, Arcs, and other lead devs in a podcast style stream in a stdio from time to time to talk about the games future?**
A: (Mr.Conway) Uhhh, sure?
(Mr.Matcha) I mean, the idea is good. But we can make, like a themed stream/podcast kind of stuff, for sure. So maybe there is a topic you would like us to cover? We can come up with something and invite the right developer to talk to you. How about that?

3.
Q: **Are any changes planned for the M60A2's HEAT shell?**
A: (Remmy) Yes. Very likely is going to lose the mini-game reload. And everything else will stay the same.

4.
Q: **If so few people end up playing the game, will you shut it down?**
A: (Mr.Conway) We are not planning to shut it down. We are planning to increase the amount of people playing the game. Which is one of the reasons why we are here. (At PGA)

5.
Q: **Any news on Raketa ult rework? It genuinely sucks now, like, worse ult in the whole game, worse than Fuzzer.**
A: (Remmy) This is funny because we nerfed it because it was too OP. We nerfed the duration, by a tiny bit. Previously you only lost 2 seconds from your ultimate if you’re getting hit whilst at one HP, and I think we made it that - if you get hit while your ultimate is active - it's going to get reduced. I spoke with a couple of people, coincidentally. We’re thinking of giving like maybe plus 2 seconds to the total duration of the ultimate. Just like an idea. Quite likely.

6.
Q: **What about a bomb mode or tournaments hosted by you guys? Also, what’s your take on 3v3 right now, and what are your plans regarding the bomb mode?**
A: (Mr.Conway) So, we are talking to a couple of guys from the community about supporting them with at least like an “invitational tournament” quite soon, so that’s something to look forward to, that you can potentially sign up to. As for PnD as a mode - I think it’s nice. The next PnD mode is also planned. We’re going to be playing it at 3v3 I think. I think it has some benefit, especially for squads to be able to really play it together. Although 5v5 can also be cool. And in general, regarding plans for the mode, I’m not sure yet. We’re testing it to potentially be headline ranked mode for example (in the future). But, whether that’s what's gonna ultimately happen depends on how things will go in the future.

7.
Q: **What about separate queue for squad vs solo players? But it would make queue times longer.**
A: (Mr.Conway) Yes, that’s one of the issues. Also, I don’t know if we can trivially adjust MM like this on the fly. It might be more difficult than it seems.

8.
Q: **A bit of a difficult question to answer. Is the development team satisfied with the performance of the game in its category? (Fast-Pace Tank-Hero Class)**
A: (Mr.Conway) I mean, I don’t think there is a category for this right? Like, is there another game in this category? Because I think one of the points of this game and the reasons it exists is because - it does its own thing, right? Which also comes with its own challenge, because it means that we have to kind of, find ways to make it really fun for people that play traditional hero-shooters and also fun for people who play traditional vehicular shooters to kind of establish our own audience in our own niche, right? So we’re kind of in the middle.

9.
Q: **(Follow-up) Yes, but you have to have a game that you are close by, You are not in the same Genre as Elden Ring for example.**
A: (Mr.Conway) We are not, exactly. We are not like Elden Ring, but that’s what I mean, like, we don’t have like a 1 to 1 comparison to say “ohh we’re super happy with this”. Are we happy with the amount of players playing the game right now? No. We definitely want more people to play the game. This is one of the reasons why we are here as well, to spread the word and convince more people to play this very cool game.

10.
Q: **What do you think about the idea of introducing "event intel"—a currency exclusive to event battle passes and rewards? These could be exchanged for boosters or converted into silver, but the balance would reset to zero at the start of each new event. In other words, players would need to log in and manually convert any remaining event intel into rewards; otherwise, it would be lost.**
A: (Mr.Conway) I think it is a fantastic idea, especially for events like Halloween one - where it’s quite short. We do have, I believe, something interesting in that regard, sort of, a little bit like that, upcoming in Season 3. I can’t talk about this yet, I don’t know how confirmed it is, but there will be something as an interesting additional motivation for playing in the season.

11.
Q: **Guys, the main issue for me is that you folks keep changing a game that’s only 3+ months old. We don’t know what to focus on, and you have little or no information or communication. The answers we get are "we’re working on it, maybe, we don’t know", etc. You’ve turned many of us loyal and veteran players off which is sad.**
A: (Mr.Conway) I would try to refute that a little bit and say that we are communicating as much as we can. We’re trying to be very transparent about what we’re testing, we’re very open in this kind of livestreams, we like to engage and if you have an idea of how we can do it more - then please let us know.
[the answers part] The problem is - I can totally blow smoke up your back side and say like “hey, absolutely, this is a fantastic suggestion, we're going to do that next month”, but the fact is that - we are still a new game, we are still finding what we really want to do, where we really see our core strengths, and we're experimenting a little bit to see what really resonates with people, cuz as you can see at the moment- - not so many people are playing, and we think that by making the game better - we can change that. And so that’s what we’re trying right now. We’re trying to communicate more about it, but the reality is - I can’t communicate to you about something that we haven’t decided yet or if I do - it won't be worth much, because we haven’t decided that yet.
We’re working on the communication part, we’re working on the speed, all of this is stuff that we are trying to address - so hopefully we would do better for you.

12.
Q: **When do we get the first bit of information for S3? Mid-November? Or get MrLeakway on the stream.**
A: (Mr.Matcha) Soon. We are working on the article that should arrive [soon]. There will be some information about S3, perhaps.

13.
Q: **Am I understanding correctly that M551 is an AP shell in stock now? If that is true, why have you done it?**
A: (Remmy) Yes, it does have AP now, as opposed to APHE. It previously had a module that gave you Zealous. It still has it, and now that combines would be changed back to APHE. The reasoning behind that was mostly because the Sheridan is a light assault, so you need to be able to go in, take out high priority targets and come back out quite fast, before someone notices you've been there. And the damage output on the APHE is not as high as AP. So the AP is there to sort of support this type of gameplay. But you still have APHE for like, more consistent damage and better tracking as well, through the progression. And it’s part of the Zealous module.

14.
Q: **Is there a way to change from HE to AP on XM1V?**
A: (Remmy) I believe not now, but I would love to give AP back to XM1V in the progression, and I will try my best to push for that, because I think AP is still a very reliable shell.

15.
Q: **With all the changes to the vehicles, roles, modules, I hope that the promise to refund our resources, so we can play and test all the vehicles properly.**
A: (Mr.Matcha) We have that in mind, and we want to make it happen. It just kind of makes sense, right?

16.
Q: **Why are you reworking everything in the game now even though there was nothing wrong with the previous system other than balancing with certain modules and tanks?**
A: **(Remmy)** It’s not so much that everything was working bad or good in the past as well, it's just a more complete overhaul of most, if not all game elements - so progression and everything. It might sound different, and it is different by all means, right now. But with this, among many things, we kinda hope to bring roles a bit more together across vehicles.

**(Mr.Matcha)** We are making this change in order to find the actual direction for the game. Previously it really felt like we didn’t have one, and we are trying to find a right position for our game as well, with these changes.

**(Remmy)** There definitely would be a lot of improvements to progression for example, since you’ve mentioned that with the modules. That will come, not too late, but it will follow for sure.

**(Mr.Matcha)** As I mentioned earlier in the stream, that this change is only a first part pretty much of what’s about to come. It’s not like one of change, and we call it a day - no. We are in the process of improving the game in the right direction, and we will be sharing more information in the coming weeks as soon as we have more concrete and confirmed information of what we’re doing and where we’re going - we will let you know. We want to be as open and as communicative about it as physically possible. So you can expect in the coming weeks another dev talk with our creative director - explaining why, what, how, and hopefully every question you could potentially have about it.

**(Flam1ngo)** Exactly. What is the reason behind that, what kind of direction it is, it will be clear to understand why we did that, what was behind the curtains.

17.
Q: **Is there anything in the talks or even in development concerning custom servers, ranked, clans, etc?**
A: (Mr.Matcha) We are aware there are features that you guys would love to have like for example, ranked and stuff. We do have them in our plans, obviously. But considering the changes we are trying to implement at the moment, unfortunately some of this had to be pushed a bit forward. We know you guys really want to have a ranked play, we really do. But there are things that unfortunately have the priority over it. This doesn’t mean it will never arrive, it’s just some things need to come first in order to make that thing happen.

18.
Q: **Yeah, understandable. Still I don't know how much patience players have to wait for such changes.**
A: (Mr.Matcha) I’m glad you understand this, and all I could ask for is some patience. All I can also say is that - we have a long term plan, a very long term plan in order to change stuff that simply, in our opinion, doesn't work at the moment. And it will require a lot of time to implement it.
(Flam1ngo) And yeah, it doesn’t mean we also shut down the game, cuz we have long term visions, plans. Unfortunately we need a little bit more time, but we are here, just we are working on it, so everything will be okay.
**[Matcha also shares that the dev talk video is coming in December]**

19.
Q: **Guys, what long term plan if completing battle-pass takes 1 week or a little longer? Why should I play casually all the time with zero motivation with the same gameplay?**
A: (Mr.Matcha) See, it’s one of the reasons we are changing things. As you pointed out yourself - you say that you don’t have motivation to play the game, and this is what we’re trying to do. We want to give you guys more ways to play, more meaningful ways to play, and this takes time to implement, and we want to simply fix it, so you guys can have more ways to play, more stuff to do, grind for things that you want to do and come back to the game. And this right here is one of the changes that we are testing today - which is the combat identity, with different classes. Having a little bit of different abilities.

20.
Q: **We’ve been waiting for the next tournament for a long time, but it still hasn’t happened.**
A: (Mr.Matcha) I might have good news tomorrow (13/11/2026) perhaps for you. If you are on the LSP server.

**TL;DR - Balance changes and bugfixes incoming, possible future content talk, merch, state of the game addressed, lot's of talk about road map (not going to come any time soon).**

For a full disclosure, this post has been reviewed (and potentially received some corrections) by Mr.Matcha before becoming public. 

Disclaimer: if you don’t see a question that interests you, there is a high probability that this question was not asked during the stream. 

Questions taken from the chat will be marked with “[C]”. 

Also, during this stream, Remmy was present in the chat and was quite actively answering the questions, so his replies are also included in this episode. 

1.
Q: **What's the activation distance on the Marder’s mines?**
A: (Mr.Conway) We are aware that whatever the activation distance is - it is probably too far right now. And in general we’re also aware that the mines are a little bit painful to deal with and that’s something that we absolutely want to improve. But unfortunately it’s taking a little bit of time because there are some non-trivial code changes that we have to make to make that happen and so you're gonna have to bear with us a little bit. But we are working on it. 
(Remmy, from the chat) targeting range for the Marder mines was 45, it will get nerfed. The cone interior angle will also be adjusted

Q: **When are we gonna get some Competitive part of the game, like rating or something? When will the "Cutted" modules(of progression) of the tanks be unlocked? When will the "Factions" or any other missions that are gonna give game resources as tech or credits?**
A: (Mr.Conway) There are obviously a few things, many things that we are still working on at the moment. There are things that have been available in testing that are not in [the game] yet. And it takes a little bit of time. And with the modules - they’ll be added slowly. We’d rather wait a little bit to make sure they’re properly balanced rather than adding them too quickly.

Q: **The most important question, why did we get the "game release" with 20% of content of gaming tests and alphas if the content and gaming code was already ready? What was the event that led to this decision?**
A: (Mr.Conway) Just because you saw it in an alpha test, doesn’t mean that it was ready-ready. 14.
Q: **Do you realize that for most players with over 100 hours in the game, content is already running low, and grinding the same quick battle gets tedious? I’m a player with 350 hours under my belt, and I find it hard to keep playing the same mode**
A: (Mr.Conway) Yes, and I absolutely agree that this is an issue. We need to give you stuff to do, things to unlock, things to achieve, things to grind for, but it is an unfortunate reality that we can’t magic these out of thin air, and you are going to have to give us time to implement them. That’s also why we are trying to do some little weekend events, something with some leader boards, to give you something that's different to play for. 

15.
Q: **When will custom matches return? They said they're still being worked on, but when should we expect them?**
A: (Mr.Conway) It’s very difficult for me to answer “when” questions, unless the “when” is really very soon and we have it confirmed, cuz until I have that confirmation - if I give you an answer - I may not be able to stick to what I’m telling you. And I really dislike having to come afterwards and say “I’m very sorry that we’re delayed [the update/feature/etc]”. That’s why you're gonna have to wait for us to be ready.

Q: **Why did the developers buff the stats of tanks that were already easy to play—like the ALVT, Marder, and FV 62AB—as if deliberately boosting the most overpowered ones? Meanwhile, vehicles like the M55A1, M1A1, or M60A1—which are sluggish for their class and difficult to use effectively—get no such buffs. While all these tanks are decent enough in quick matches, if you introduce a ranked system, only 5 or 6 of them will actually see play because the rest simply aren't competitive.**
A: (Mr.Conway) Generally, our goal is to have all tanks be competitive and be balanced … Our intention was always to balance things. Sometimes things go a little bit too far, and I know that especially ALVT and Marder are in a very strong spot right now because of some of the abilities, quirks and how they work. This is something that we will absolutely address. We are working on an update for this and we will keep you informed as to when we can deploy it. We’re doing our best to do this as quickly as possible.

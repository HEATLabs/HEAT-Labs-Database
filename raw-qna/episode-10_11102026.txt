**TL;DR - some interesting information regarding S3 is coming soon, P&D in an updated form coming this weekend (3v3 instead of 5v5), some form of roadmap is being worked on, referral programme is being worked on, QoL features are currently top priority, old skins potentially making a return, next stream will feature a new guest. **

This episode covers three Wednesday streams on **23rd of September**, **30th of September** and **7th of October**.

For a full disclosure, this post has been reviewed (and potentially received some corrections) by Mr.Matcha before becoming public.

Disclaimer: if you don’t see a question that interests you, there is a high probability that this question was not asked during the stream.

Repeated questions, reiterated public information and vague answers were not included in the episode.

1.
Q: **Don’t want to seem like a broken record, but it would be great if your development team would put together a roadmap. Then we could actually plan on what to work on rather than wasting precious time and resources. With all due respect I ask.**
A: (Mr.Conway) I get you, I feel you. We are going to be talking about some cool things coming in Season 3, in like a week or two. We’re still finishing internal testing, so there is going to be some interesting news coming, but it’s a little bit early to share it right now.

2.
Q: **Can we hope for a PnD return soon? Ranked will probably not be here soon, no?**
A: (Mr.Conway) Yes, we have a P&D weekend coming up soon, in a few weeks. And before this P&D weekend comes we are actually going to make a small change to P&D that you may enjoy. This change will be at the next mini update, so you have to wait a little bit for P&D.

3.
Q: **We should have rolling weekend events, different events each weekend with leader boards and rewards to encourage gameplay.**
A: (Mr.Conway) Almost doing this at the moment. We’ve kinda trying to do something, at least every second weekend. Maybe it’s worth having a discussion whether it should be every weekend.

4.
Q: **Return of 20v20 on weekend?**
A: (Mr.Conway) I think 20v20 needs a little bit of cooking, based on the feedback from everybody been saying, 20v20 needs a bit of change to the points, matches are a bit too quick, sometimes can be too chaotic.

5.
Q: **We need people who can make decisions. Still no roadmap, many balance changes were overturned or worthless. Nothing has changed. Discord is only whining and toxic. Needs moderators to manage it. We have 142 people watching here. Almost our entire player base. Is anyone really listen but even more importantly what is being done?**
A: (Mr.Matcha) So, when it comes to the roadmap, there are a few issues about it, because in the game development unfortunately there are a bunch of things that are floating around and can change pretty much on a daily basis, and we don’t want to commit to something without knowing what can actually be delivered on that specific time. I would really love to have it, I’m actually trying to at least give you a short roadmap. I’m actually working on it and I want to make sure that, if it gets approved of course; to give you something to look into the very near future, so please bear with us. Well I would say - if you don’t like something specific, please always share your feedback with us. We know we just released the patch last week, and it’s a fresh one; we always count on your opinions guys, so always through it at us, we read it with Remmy, with Lynx, from now on - with Flam1ngo as well. And I actually make weekly feedback reports that I send to our developers. So, if there is something that you want to see improved or changed, or fixed, always let us know, because - without you telling us - there is nothing we can do.

6.
Q: **Sorry, that’s the same response for 3 months. We keep telling you with all due respect. It’s not your fault at all. It’s. It’s not even the developers. It’s management.**
A: (Mr.Matcha) I know it’s the same response that we keep giving, but that's just the reality of the game dev.     Unfortunately. I wish things were better, were easier to communicate, faster to develop, but there is not always that you can do these things, unfortunately. We’re really try to do as much as we can.

7.
Q:  **In that 20on20 format, there should be an opportunity to gain 3000 conquest points to collect, otherwise the battle is quick over.**
A: (Mr.Matcha) I do agree, and we are aware of the problem with battles ending quickly. We will definitely change this. We don’t know when will be the next 20v20 opportunity to play, but there will be eventually. And soon there will be another event coming!

8.
Q: **Are there community-testers now? I have a lot of experience in game-tests... Perhaps I can be useful.**
A: (Flam1ngo) For sure. LSP is still open?
(Mr.Matcha) Yes, yes! Reach out to me or Flam1ngo and we will get you sorted. There should be a fairly big test coming soon. We always appreciate the feedback. The more - the better.

9.
Q: **What about an idea to "refer a friend" to heat with some bonus code logic**
A: (Mr.Matcha) Actually, I am responsible person to push this feature because I was the one who suggested that about a month and a half ago, and it went silent, but then my manager came and said “oh actually, we should get it done”. Aaaaand guess who is responsible for it - a person who suggested it. So, I’m working on it. It takes a little bit of time because I need to do a little bit of research and see the technicalities of how it can be implemented, what rewards we can implement as well for referrals. So, we’ll see, but it will eventually come.

10.
Q: **Would a free for all mode ever work for this game?**
A: (Mr.Matcha) I think it will be a chaos, and it would be quite hard to balance, because in some situations the TTK in HEAT is actually quite high, but in fights when you have like 2v1, you’re already in a huge disadvantage, so it will be interesting to see how it would play out. Surely it would be nice to have maybe like an April fools kind of event going on. That would be pretty cool.

11.
Q: **What's your plan to grow the game, I feel like the core community enjoys the game, but we need more people.**
A: (Mr.Matcha) So, first of all we have regular meetings on QoL improvements with our developers. We try to prioritize things and help make these decisions. Obviously some features take more time to implement, some are more difficult, some are less, but you also need to prioritize what is actually important for the health of the game as well. Something that might be easy to implement might not necessarily be a priority to include and other things are just simply more important to implement and work on. We do have a list of improvements that were mentioned on stream with Rickles, and it is coming.

12.
Q: **It's been a while since I brought up this topic but, previous skins from alphas have been brought back for some vehicles in a new colour scheme. Can we see the previous ones coming back as a colour variation of said skins even if it costs money? I would die to get A1 and A2 Abrams and xm1 90 skins again.**
A: (Mr.Matcha) So, I see it, honestly within the community team we are trying to plant the seeds if we could potentially somehow bring those back. My perfect idea would be to have it like a unique super skin that changes everything about the agents and gives these Alpha look. What do you think about it?

13.
Q: **How soon will the bug fixes for the new modules be released?**
A: (Mr.Matcha) I’m not sure. I delivered your feedback this week, so it will take some time to check it out. But it has been delivered to the devs.

14.
Q: **Remove kill confirmed.**
A: (Mr.Conway) We are discussing the KC situation, we had to look into it and chat with devs. At the moment, it does appear a little bit too much in the matchmaker, so that’s for sure something we’re going to address.

15.
Q: **Ignoring the elephant in the room questions makes those of us who love and support the game disenchanted to say the least. Your answers are the same for 3 months. “you can’t say” or working on it means nothing. Silence is deafening.**
A: (Mr.Conway) I fully understand, and I wish I could answer your questions differently, I really am not trying to avoid that. But game development is surprisingly very difficult and sometimes these things take more time than we would like to. We have a limited amount of developers that are working on a whole bunch of things, and we have a backlog of things we need to work through, but we can’t always prioritize things that everybody wants.

16.
Q: **Planned to add Marks of Excellence on the gun barrels to the game, like in the original World of Tanks?**
A: (Mr.Conway) We do not have a concrete plan for that yet. MoEs are one of my favourite features, to be honest; in WoT. I really like it. Beautiful long-term progression goal. But I think we have a bigger fish to fry at the moment.

17.
Q: **When will the new window be PnD?**
A: (Mr.Conway) It’s this weekend, and an announcement tomorrow (08/10/2026) with details.

18.
Q: **Game files show that the bomb mode will be 3v3. Don't you think that's a bad idea? Why did they remove 5v5? For better matchmaking?**
A: (Mr.Conway) Yes, it’s going to be 3v3. Why? It’s because we like to experiment. The P&D was run as an experiment, and we wanted to try something different.

19.
Q: **Are you not planning to release more content per season? The game isn't exactly packed with styles and agents for a three-month period.**
A: (Mr.Conway) Yes. We’re definitely trying to release more and more content as we go along, and I think that overall speed will pick up a little bit once we have some of the other things sorted.

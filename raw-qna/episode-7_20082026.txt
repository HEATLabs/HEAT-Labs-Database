**TL;DR - lot’s of QoL talk: ults QoL, zoom, targeting, future leader board improvements, a bit of balance talk, clans being worked on although no ETA since they need to make some sort of content for it. **

For a full disclosure, this post has been reviewed (and potentially received some corrections) by Mr.Matcha before becoming public. 

Disclaimer: if you don’t see a question that interests you, there is a high probability that this question was not asked during the stream.  

Repeated questions, reiterated public information and vague answers were not included in the episode.  

1.
Q: **This leader board doesn't make any sense.**
A: (Remmy) We’re working on improving it. Right now it tracks your average score. We’re trying to see how to improve it. 

2.
Q: **Will there be an ability to see your stats per tank?**
A: (Remmy) Perhaps. It’s something that's come up. Granted - a small amount of times. Perhaps in relation to the leader board itself, if that is what you are referring to.

3.
Q: **Why do some ult get lost when you are killed while using them (Like reaper) but others don't (Like Kent's)?**
A: (Remmy) Great question. What I am pushing for us to do right now is a bit more consistency across our ultimate functionalities. So you’ll notice that if you cancel Kent ulti, it will go down to zero, if you cancel all other ultis before you actually cast them, they will just go back to full charge. One thing that I hope we will do and I’m kinda pushing for it - is that so you might be able to cancel ulti in the future and have it back to 100% as opposed to it wasting. But that should mean that if you activate it but don’t use it and you get destroyed in the meantime then it’ll probably get wasted, idk. We’ll see what we’ll do with that. 

4.
Q: **Any plans about being able to zoom in with Reaper ult?**
A: (Remmy) [proceeds to write it down] I will find out for you. If it happens for Reaper ulti, it would also need to happen for Fuzzer ulti for example (which already have that, btw). What I also maybe like to improve about Fuzzer specifically is a 3 second lock after you cast your ability on an enemy or an ally. You are stuck in the targeting camera for 3 seconds. Which sucks, and I don’t like it, so I will look into it.

**TL:DR - AFK problem addressed, more console fixes are coming, modding support is in talks but nothing decisive yet, Saturday tournament stream announcement, Sheridan and T62 rebalancing are in works.**

For a full disclosure, this post has been reviewed (and potentially received some corrections) by Mr.Matcha before becoming public. 

Disclaimer: if you don’t see a question that interests you, there is a high probability that this question was not asked during the stream. 

Repeated questions, reiterated public information and vague answers were not included in the episode. 

1.
Q: **Why is it by date? Why is it always unavailable? (about plant/defuse)**
A: (Mr.Matcha) We see it as a “testing purpose”. We count on your feedback, what works, what doesn’t, so we can adjust, fix things before we can fully launch it. 

2.
Q: **Will Leo 2ST receive hiding from Thermo Vision on invis?**
A: (Remmy) I don’t know. I’ve asked, but I don't think I received an answer. 

3.
Q: **Are optimizations coming to PC? [Intel Core] i7 is struggling with this game.**
A: (Remmy) It might be a bug, it might be an actual issue. What would help us - if you could reach out to our support team. They might ask for exact details. They might also be able to provide you with an answer as to - yes it’s a problem with the game, or maybe it's a problem with the device, which I doubt.

4.
Q: **A few points that have been raised by viewers: The ability to switch between NA/EU/ASIA server hosts as game ping varies according to who is hosting in a squad. Account botting perhaps to farm tech points seems to be a new issue we have noticed in games (due to tanks being in game, but not moving nor disconnected or AFK)**
A: (Remmy) The first question - I’m not sure of. The second one - we’re aware of this and also not aware of this. We’re aware that AFK is a problem right now, and we’re working on some issues, and then if someone is AFK, they’re becoming a free score for the enemy, so that’s something we’re working on. I actually discussed it recently with the man himself - Warpurple

5.
Q: **Why doesn’t the Friends window show whether a friend has accepted the squad invite?**
A: (Remmy) Is it on the Xbox you’re referring to? And if so - we are aware of that issue. It’s been an issue for a while, though I kinda remember it being fixed, but apparently maybe not. I’ve seen it from another player in Discord. So I’m not sure if it’s the same Xbox issue. 

6.
Q: **Given the current online player count, have you considered reducing the number of 10v10 battles? Quite often, during early mornings or late at night, matches end up being 7v7 to 9v9 with real players, and the remaining slots are filled with bots, which makes the gameplay frustrating.**
A: (Remmy) Of course our prime times, for example in Europe. At certain times during the evening you might see a lower drop, because people are sleeping, so you may encounter some bots there. Of course, it’s not always going to be this way, we’re aware of this, we’re working on it, but it can happen.

7.
Q: **Matchmaker needs a rework as well, for example in my current match, enemy team has XM1V, and we don't have this imbalance in certain game modes such as hardpoint or conquest**
A: (Remmy) Matchmaker is something we’re paying very close attention to, and hopefully we will improve on.

8.
Q: **The issue comes with XM1V laying mines on a cap circle and other team not having a tank capable of clearing the mines, this why you get imbalance**
A: (Remmy) That’s something actually we’re trying to adjust a little bit through other vehicles (engineering?) I will not say more to it, because it’s another test we’re doing right now with a certain vehicle that Rickles is working on. Rickles being one of our designers. Nothing final, but it might be something that will be adjusted slightly. 
**(Note from OP: Rickles confirmed earlier that he is working on an M1150 ABV engineering vehicle that will be Ember’s second tank. He also shared that it is his last vehicle he's going to work on, as he is working on core game systems, and he simply doesn’t have enough time to do vehicles as well)**

9.
Q: **Are you going to be doing cinematics for new tanks now that they are turned off by default?**
A: (Remmy) I don’t know. I don’t think we have any plans at the moment, at least that I know of. It would be cool, in the future to continue this, because the cinematics are awesome, but maybe in the future. 

10.
Q: **Remmy is cheesing the 2fk aim ability (in regard to by-passing the 2FK’s ability by not looking directly at him)**
A: (Remmy) It’s a known thing that you can move your reticle away from the 2FK and then move it back, and you’ll hit it, and you're not going to be affected by the jammers. Kinda intended also not intended at the same time. Until that gets fixed, and I dare to say maybe it’ll get fixed but there is no decisive decision on that at the moment, so it seems like a good thing to counter the vehicle at the moment.

11.
Q: **What about the armor layout on the console? When will it be fixed?**
A: (Remmy) Soon. I’ve already added it to a certain patch notes. 

12.
Q: **Don't see why it should be fixed (in regard to 2FK’s ability). Maybe make the window smaller before the bloom, but it's a fair counterplay tbf.**
A: (Remmy) Because it’s a direct counter to the ability, and it was not originally intended. Right now it’s a workaround but not quite.

13.
Q:** Did you buff the movement of the FV?**
A: (Remmy) We’re testing a couple of things, I don’t think there are any movement [adjustments] planned. There are other extra functionalities that we’re playing around with, but I don't think movement is one of them. 

14.
Q: **What's up with the introduced bug with the base cap icon color? Like you decap a base, and it's showing your team blue color lowering with the enemy red color as the background. Irritating.**
A: (Remmy) It’s known. Actually one of our UI designers reported it. It’s known and it will be fixed. 

15.
Q: **Any plans on supporting and giving more access to information that game carries for the modding community - both the stats and for example UI restyles?**
A: (Mr.Matcha) If I’m not mistaken there is this discussion going on right now, there is nothing set in stone, but I think the idea has been thrown by the community team if I’m correct. I remember our colleague - Lynx was trying to get something out of it, so I know that there is something going on, but nothing is set in stone.

Remmy also shares that he knows people are hating on Raketa in KC. He says that **“We are already looking into T62, and we’ve actually play tested some changes we want to make, internally. Nothing final, of course, but I dare to say - we are on the right track with getting Raketa back in line”**. Later he also yapped for solid 2 or so minutes about **M60A2** and **Sheridan**, specifying that the ladder will get buffed, and the main thing they are looking at (at his moment) is mobility, to make him **“more nimble”** and bring him overall in-line with HSTV-L. A bit later he also adds that they are also testing a few other changes to Sheridan, to see what will work well, and hull traverse is one of the changes they are testing. He specifies that **doesn’t mean that sheridan will get a hull traverse speed**, rather that the **devs are aware of it**, and are **working on Sheridan** to see how they play out.

Matcha also made an announcement that, this **Saturday (15/08/2026) at 14:00 UTC (approx)**, they will have a **showcase tournament** in plant and defuse. Unfortunately, this time at least, the tournament itself is **exclusive to LSP players**, while others will be able to see it through stream, where **all 3 CMs (Remmy, MrMatcha and MrConway)** will be commentating on it.

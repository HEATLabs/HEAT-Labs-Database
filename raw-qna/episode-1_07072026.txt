**TL;DR - Balance talk, modules, Marder and ALVT nerf incoming, QoL, leader boards, CC programme requirements revealed, other smaller stuff.**

This episode covers 2 Wednesday streams as well as 2 TankFest streams. 

For a full disclosure, this post has been reviewed (and potentially received some corrections) by Mr.Matcha before becoming public.

Disclaimer: if you don’t see a question that interests you, there is a high probability that this question was not asked during the stream. 

As always, repeated questions were not included in this post (there were quite a lot of them)

1.

But if a balance change isn't in this season - doesn't mean it's not going to be in the next one for example.

22.
Q: **How is the game SO unbalanced after so many years of testing? Were you guys rushed to release the game?**
A: (Mr.Conway) So, first of all, I have an issue with your assumption that the game is SO unbalanced. There are definitely improvements to be made, but overall I think the game is actually in a pretty decent state right now and the improvements will be made, so there will be things that you'll feel, don't worry. But the main problem is that it's impossible to balance an online video game without being fully online and without players being in your game. It's necessary. 

People have different ways of playing, different ways of interacting with the game, and you can't get the data to really refine balancing until you get a huge amount of people playing over time, because over time people get progression, they learn, they get better, they figure out what works, what doesn't work, and then you can start making some fantastic conclusions about what is balanced, what isn't, and what needs to be tweaked.

Q: **[this question was a part of a bigger discussion in chat]...The developers could roll out a new game mode in less than 24h. They have a team of at least 200 ppl .... I could probably do it myself in a week. They're just dragging it out for no reason. When you have a team of 200 people, the question isn't "Can we make a new mode?" The question is "Which mode should we make"**
A: (Mr.Conway) So, first of all you have to understand that if you have a team of 200, right? Not all of those people are technically able to make a game mode. Then we are also working on things already and while we can absolutely make a prototype of a game mode, very quickly, and we do; and we iterate it very quickly with those, that doesn't mean that you can make a production-ready "I can release it to the game" game mode in that time.

26.
Q: **[From the same guy, replying to the comment in the chat] ...Players have been conditioned to accept waiting for vague, undefined updates in almost every game. People now treat long delays as normal, even when many features or game modes could realistically be developed much faster. That's why expectations are so low. Instead of asking "why something takes so long", people just accept it. Meanwhile, the quality of communication, the developers' responsiveness to the community, and the number of logical decisions seem to k...[?... he did not continue his complaint]**
A: (Mr.Conway) Game development is not easy. Making a game, especially an online multi-player game is non-trivial exercise. It's quite hard work. And we really want to make sure that the incidents like when we launched don't happen. 

We want [the game] to be stable, we want [the game] to be good quality. And doing all of that, getting all the little bits and bobs into something that makes like a full game mode, not just a prototype that kinda works and looks junky; but like a mode that works well, has UI, UX, sound support, the rewards work, the balancing works, all of that takes time. And we're working on it, and we're going to do as much as we can as quickly as we can, but it just takes time. And there is a lot of this...

Like if you have one cook making a soup, it'll take a certain amount of time and maybe if you have two cooks making soup, it'll go a bit quicker. But if I have 10 cooks making soup - probably won't make any difference, and they'll get in each other's way. And it holds true when it comes to game development as well. You can't have 200 ppl all working on 1 game mode, because it'll be an absolute mess. So, we do our best to communicate with you guys, right? We do this stream every week, I try to make sure that we are very open.

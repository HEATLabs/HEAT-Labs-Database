**TL;DR - Balancing discussion, chat being worked on, QoL features, longshot xd being worked on.**

For a full disclosure, this post has been reviewed (and potentially received some corrections) by Mr.Matcha before becoming public. 

Disclaimer: if you don’t see a question that interests you, there is a high probability that this question was not asked during the stream. 

Repeated questions, reiterated public information and vague answers were not included in the episode. 

1.
Q: **Why was 2FK improved, but not 2KST improved? 2FK was good, so why touch it and turn it into an imba?**
A: (Remmy) The changes we’ve done were to HEAT guided and non-guided ATGMs, so technically the same adjustments that 2FK got [were applied to other vehicles that use HEAT]

2.
Q: **Are you going to fix 2KST's hull traverse, boost and AP rounds, which are obviously bugged?**
A: (Remmy) For a boost - it might be a bug, I know what you mean exactly. The other ones are not “bugs” so to say. AP module will get adjusted. Hull traverse - maybe. We’ll see. But it does turn a little bit like a cruise ship.

3.
Q: **When is the new content planned at least approximately? There is nothing to do in the game, everything is upgraded and bypassed, the combat pass was completed in 10 minutes after the update, and the 2KST and 2FK tanks were upgraded in a few days, the game became boring.**
A: (Remmy) It will come. Soon. More patches and season updates are coming. 
(Mr.Matcha) Yeah, these things take time. We physically cannot produce so much content. It takes time, especially when we have multiple platforms as well. 

4.
Q: **Will new modules be added to existing tanks?**
A: (Remmy) Yes, in the future. 

5.
Q: **Will there be custom lobbies and statistics in the second season?**
A: (Mr.Matcha) I don’t think in the second season at least. But, in the near future we definitely want to have something competitive for you guys. 

6.
Q: **When there will be an in-game chat?**
A: (Remmy) Soon, we hope. 

7.
Q: **Longshot XP fix when?**
A: (Remmy) We know it. It’s being worked on. 

8.
Q: **Will the game feature modern post-Cold War vehicles, similar to how they are implemented in the console version of WoT? I’d love to see vehicles like the T-80, Leopard 2A5, and M1A2, all of which are present in the console version;?**
A: (Remmy) I don’t know, the current roster of the vehicles we have is not final. More will come. What those tanks will look like is still yet to be decided. But we have a lot of tanks in mind that we want to add. So, stay tuned. More will come. 

9.
Q: **So, not fixing the descriptions and not letting us know what each gun/module is doing?**
A: (Remmy) I know some bugs where the descriptions are either wrong or not totally accurate. It will be fixed. 

10.
Q: **When will you introduce a premium tank which earns more of everything?**
A: (Remmy) Never. 

11.
Q: **Any planned ranked mode coming?**
A: (Remmy) Yes. It’s being worked on. When [it’s going to be released] - we cannot say yet. Not because we don’t want to but because we want to make sure that if we do give everyone a date - we can make sure we deliver.

12.
Q: **I've been having fun and the new tanks are very fun to fight against. It is interesting about the frontal armor of the new leo (2KST), I didn't expect it to be so strong.**
A: (Remmy) 2KST has some frontal armor that's a bit annoying to deal with depending on who you are, but that will be adjusted. 

13.
Q: **Are you guys going to change the squad a bit? It’s kinda confusing to add people or at least an in game fried list?**
A: (Remmy) It’s being worked on. 

14.
Q: **We really need battle replays; will this be introduced soon?**
A: (Remmy) I don’t know if soon, but definitely yes. 

15.
Q: **QoL question: The 'last' zoom level on pressing the zoom button kinda bugs me every time. Any plans of introducing a default zoom level and lock the zoom mode only on press instead of scroll?**
A: (Remmy) I know what you mean, I will pass it on.

16.
Q: **Why do you guys think ALVT needs a nerf?**
A: (Remmy) It has crazy damage output and reloads. But it will very, very, very, likely lose the Zealous from the modules.
